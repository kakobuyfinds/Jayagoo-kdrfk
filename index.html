Jayagoo spreadsheet shopping guide

Build a shortlist you can actually use.

01

Choose a useful starting category

Begin with shoes, hoodies, T-shirts, or accessories. Narrowing the category makes it easier to compare products with similar uses and sizing requirements.

02

Record the exact listing details

Save the product URL, color, size, stated material, currency, and date checked. Separate what the seller states from what you have independently verified.

03

Check the full purchase conditions

Review shipping, applicable fees, delivery restrictions, and return terms. Recheck availability and price on the destination page before placing an order.

What is a Jayagoo spreadsheet?

The phrase refers to a product-list format used to organize shopping finds, such as item links, categories, sizes, prices, and notes. This page is an independent browsing guide, not an official Jayagoo spreadsheet or ordering service.

Can I download a spreadsheet here?

This page provides a browsing guide and comparison checklist. It does not offer an XLSX or CSV download. Category links open CAFANS listings.

What should I check before choosing a product?

Check the exact variant, garment measurements or shoe size chart, materials, current price, available stock, delivery terms, and return policy. Listing photos alone do not establish authenticity or quality.

Does a listed price include shipping and taxes?

Do not assume it does. Verify shipping charges, service fees, currency conversion, and applicable import charges before paying. Costs depend on the seller, checkout, destination, and delivery method.

Is this an official Jayagoo or brand website?

No. This is an independent guide directing readers to CAFANS. Brand names are used as editorial references; no affiliation, endorsement, or product authentication is claimed.
